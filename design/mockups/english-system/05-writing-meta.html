03

각 문단의 역할은 무엇이었지?

좋은 글은 문단마다 분명한 *역할*을 갖는다. 아래 라벨을 각 문단으로 끌어 놓고, 흐름이 자연스러운지 확인해보자. 2 / 5
01 P1. "Climate change is one of the most serious problems… individuals also have important roles." CLAIM / 주장
02 P2. "First, we can reduce our energy use… turning off the lights… using public transportation…" EVIDENCE / 근거
03 P3. "Second, we should change our consumption habit… Buying eco-friendly products…" 라벨 놓기
04 P4. "Finally, education is essential. If we teach children about environment from a young age…" 라벨 놓기
05 P5. "In conclusion, although the government has the biggest power, every individual's small action can make a difference." 라벨 놓기
